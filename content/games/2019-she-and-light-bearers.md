**Description** :  
She and the Light Bearer is a point and click adventure game, a poem, a fairy tale. Take a journey inside a vibrantly colored forest, meet silly creatures, listen to serene folk music, and discover somber secrets.

**Released** : 2019

**Platform** :
- [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/658590/She_and_the_Light_Bearer/)

**Tech** :
- Unity (C#)
- Fungus (Unity framework for the narrative/visual novel parts)

**My Role** : Contract Programmer at Mojiken Studio

I worked with Mojiken Studio as a contract programmer for two years on this game. The story runs on Fungus, a Unity framework for visual novels, but the game needed more interaction than a traditional visual novel offers. I designed and built several mini-games and connected them to the Fungus dialogue system, so the puzzles and the story flow together. The game was released on **Steam**.
