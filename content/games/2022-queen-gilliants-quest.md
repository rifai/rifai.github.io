**Description** :  
Queen Gilliant's Quest is an educational game about Ratios and Proportional Relationships.

**Released** : 2022

**Platform** :
- [HTML5](https://games.legendsoflearning.com/games/WyJnYW1lcyIsMjgwNV0=?noSignup=true)

**Tech** :
- Unity (C#)

**My Role** : Lead Programmer

We made this game for [Legends of Learning](https://legendsoflearning.com), a game-based learning platform, to teach Ratios and Proportional Relationships. [Crescentyr](https://crescentyr.com) handled the game design and art, and I programmed the whole game in Unity, from the first prototype to the version released on the platform.
