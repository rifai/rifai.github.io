**Description** :  
Angry Birds Eggstraction is an educational game that uses Angry Birds to teach Collisions and Forces.

**Released** : 2024

**Platform** :
- [HTML5](https://games.legendsoflearning.com/game/angry-birds-eggstraction/3623?partner=legends-public&media=game)

**Tech** :
- Unity (C#)

**My Role** : Lead Programmer

This game came out of a partnership between [Legends of Learning](https://legendsoflearning.com) and **Rovio** to bring Angry Birds into the classroom. The challenge was to teach Collisions and Forces while keeping the feel that makes Angry Birds fun. [Crescentyr](https://crescentyr.com) handled the game design and art, and I programmed the whole game in Unity, from the first prototype to its release as an officially licensed Angry Birds game.
