**Description** :  
Bubble Tanks - Idle Defense is an idle tower defense game where you grow a small bubble tower into the strongest tower in the galaxy by fighting off waves of enemy bubbles and unlocking new weapons, skills, and upgrades.

**Released** : 2024

**Platform** :
- [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.abersoft.bubble.tower)
- [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bubble-tanks-idle-defense/id6608965183)

**Tech** :
- Unity (C#)

**My Role** : Lead Programmer

I worked with [Abersoft](https://abersoft.se) on Bubble Tanks - Idle Defense, an incremental tower defense game for mobile. As Lead Programmer, I built the game in Unity, including the core defense gameplay, the upgrade and progression systems, and the release on both Android and iOS.
