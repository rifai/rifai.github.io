**Description** :  
Cut the Rope: Forces and Motion is an educational game where students deliver candy to Om Nom using push, pull, and gravity.

**Released** : 2025

**Platform** :
- HTML5

**Tech** :
- Unity (C#)

**My Role** : Lead Programmer

This game came out of a partnership between [Legends of Learning](https://legendsoflearning.com) and **ZeptoLab** to bring Cut the Rope into the classroom. The challenge was to teach push, pull, and gravity while keeping the playful puzzle feel of the original. [Crescentyr](https://crescentyr.com) handled the game design and art, and I programmed the whole game in Unity, from the first prototype to its release as an officially licensed Cut the Rope game.
