**Description** :  
Geometry Tank - Dash Adventure is an arena shooter where you pilot a tank across an endless grid of arenas, blast waves of geometric enemies, and collect crystals to evolve from a tiny Class 1 tank into a massive Class 6 mothership, or stay small and boost your weapons instead.

**Released** : 2026

**Platform** :
- [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.abersoft.geometry.tank)
- [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/geometry-tank-dash-adventure/id6761611757)

**Tech** :
- Unity (C#)

**My Role** : Lead Programmer

I worked with [Abersoft](https://abersoft.se) on Geometry Tank - Dash Adventure, an arena shooter for mobile. As Lead Programmer, I built the game in Unity, including the tank combat, the class evolution and crystal systems, the endless arena grid, and the release on both Android and iOS.
