**Description** :  
Influencer Girl Hollywood Life is a fashion and life-sim game where you customize your avatar and rise from newcomer to Hollywood star, unlocking outfits, hairstyles, and makeup, attending photoshoots and red carpet events, and travelling to iconic locations as your follower count grows.

**Released** : 2026

**Platform** :
- [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.abersoft.influencer.girl)
- [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/influencer-girl-hollywood-life/id6756261474)

**Tech** :
- Unity (C#)

**My Role** : Lead Programmer

I worked with [Abersoft](https://abersoft.se) on Influencer Girl Hollywood Life, a fashion and celebrity life-sim game for mobile. As Lead Programmer, I built the game in Unity, including avatar customization, the fame and progression systems, and the release on both Android and iOS.
